About me

I am a third-year undergraduate for a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science at the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, in the Faculty of Applied Sciences. I came into the degree through the Physical Science (Mathematics) stream at Royal College, Colombo 07, and that grounding still shapes how I work — I would rather understand why something behaves the way it does than memorise the steps.

Most of my work so far has been full-stack: a microservices platform built with React, Spring Boot and Docker, database-driven web applications in PHP and MySQL, and desktop software in Java and C#. Alongside the degree I have completed diplomas in ICT and in Human Resource Management, which means I think about the people a system is for as much as the architecture behind it.
